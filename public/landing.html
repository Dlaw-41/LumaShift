Researching light-sensitive spaces

When overhead light is a trigger, your room should not be.

LumaShift is a renter-friendly overhead-fixture retrofit being developed for people whose migraines or light sensitivity are aggravated by bright, fluorescent, or standard LED overhead lighting. No rewiring, electrician, or landlord approval.

The problem is specific

Three things overhead lighting can get wrong.

This is research, not a medical treatment. We are looking at how a fixture can reduce exposure to common environmental triggers in the places people live and work.

01

Glare

Light pointed directly at your eyes can feel sharp and exhausting. LumaShift redirects segments toward a wall instead.

02

Flicker

Some LED drivers create imperceptible but disruptive flicker. We are designing around a better driver.

03

Blue-heavy light

Many overhead LEDs lean cool and blue. LumaShift is being designed around a warmer color temperature.

Why this is different

Wearables help in the moment. Static filters cannot adapt.

Glasses can travel with you, but they do not change the light in your space. Fixed covers can soften a fixture, but they cannot direct light where you need it. LumaShift is an adjustable, renter-friendly retrofit intended to address glare, flicker, and color temperature together.

We are validating this problem with people who live with it. We are not making treatment, cure, pricing, or clinical-performance claims.
